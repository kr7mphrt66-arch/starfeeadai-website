Frequently Asked Questions

Why are food analysis results estimates?

StarFeed AI uses AI-assisted analysis based on the image and available information. Portion size, ingredients, and cooking methods may affect the accuracy of the result.

How can I request account or data deletion?

You can submit an account or personal data deletion request by emailing our support address.

Does StarFeed AI provide medical advice?

No. StarFeed AI is intended for general informational and nutrition tracking purposes and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

What should I send when reporting a problem?

Please include your device model, operating system version, a short description of the issue, and a screenshot that does not contain sensitive personal information if possible.
